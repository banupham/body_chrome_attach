# BODY ↔ Brain contract v1 — physical boundary

Authority order: HUMAN > GUARDIAN > BRAIN > BODY.

## BODY responsibility

BODY is only:
1. eyes — return observable browser/page/UI facts;
2. hands — execute one requested physical action;
3. transport/routing — maintain browser, extension, tab and request correlation.

BODY must not decide target quality, retry, next action, goal success, safety policy, device eligibility or Guardian policy.

BODY_OBSERVE_RESULT must not contain:

- correct/wrong;
- shouldRetry;
- nextAction/recommendedAction.

## BODY_STEP_RESULT

For Brain actions BODY reports only physical execution facts:
- accepted
- attemptCount
- replayed
- dispatched
- completed
- commandId
- plannedLowLevelSteps
- completedLowLevelSteps
- technical error
- observation.before
- observation.after
- observable changes

Goal-level interpretation belongs to Brain.

## Guardian boundary

Guardian is external. BODY does not evaluate Guardian policy and Guardian does not gate Brain tasks or BODY_STEP execution.

Guardian protects two things only:
- Browser validity: invalid Chrome/browser instances are disconnected at the browser/session boundary.
- Human-learning provenance: when third-party automation or suspicious input is detected, HUMAN recorder events are not fed into BODY learning.

BODY still observes raw events. Brain task execution remains unchanged. Guardian disconnect makes HUMAN learning fail closed until Guardian returns.

Human local control remains the highest authority.

## CDP freeze

The stable motor path remains unchanged. This split must not modify the frozen CDP/motor files.
